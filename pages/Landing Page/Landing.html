FOLIO

by Anne Reforma

I'm Anne Reforma! a.k.a Lumicauwu ♥
I am 21 years old, a digital artist from the Philippines.
I started making digital artworks at the age of 13 and I've continued on further studying it as a college student pursuing multimedia art (MMA).
I've started with creating edits from a game called Gacha Club, I felt like this is where I found my dream and no matter what people say in regards of my likeness with this game, I will forever wholeheartedly cherish it and will never be afraid to say that I have started in such bizarre game.
From learning in creating small drawing edits of the characters, to rigging and animating characters, creation of stories, motion graphics and more, I have found myself longing to draw and to project my feelings in those pieces.
I hope that to the younger generation who's longing to pursue arts will never be frightened of what people may have to say. There is more to the arts than of what we know.
